You check your phone dozens of times a day. Vocabi turns some of those glances into new vocabulary.

Add a Vocabi widget to your home screen and it shows a word in the language you're learning, with its meaning in the language you already speak just below. No app to open, no streak to keep up. You learn the word while you're on your way to something else.

WORDS RIGHT ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
• Small, medium and large widgets. The large one adds an example sentence that shows the word in use.
• Add as many widgets as you like. Give each one its own slot and every widget shows a different word, never the same one twice.
• Tap a word to open its full card in the app.

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Get a new word every hour, every day or every week.
• Or choose tap mode: tap the widget and the next word appears without opening the app.

FOUR LANGUAGES, ANY PAIR
• English, Turkish, French and Spanish.
• Choose the language you speak and the one you want to learn, in any combination. Turkish to French, Spanish to English, English to Turkish and so on.

WORDS THAT FIT YOUR LIFE
• Beginner, intermediate and advanced levels. Mix them however you like.
• Topics: business, school, daily life, travel, technology, health and food.
• Hundreds of hand-picked words with clear definitions and example sentences.

QUIZ YOURSELF, THEN COMPETE
• The quiz uses the words your widgets have already shown you, so every question checks something you've actually seen.
• Ten multiple-choice questions per round. Answer fast for bonus points.
• Post your score to the global leaderboard and see how you rank today, this week and this month.

GO FURTHER WITH VOCABI PRO (OPTIONAL)
• AI word packs: fresh words for your level and topics, refreshed continuously.
• Custom topics you type yourself ("sailing", "job interviews"), up to 5.
• Unlimited vocabulary. Everything in the free version stays free.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads and no tracking.
• The leaderboard only shows the nickname you choose. Scores are stored in Apple's iCloud.
• Pro word packs are requested from our server without any personal data.
